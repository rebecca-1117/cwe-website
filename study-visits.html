Service line 04 · Study visits & short programmes

Short programmes that open doors in both directions

Study-abroad exchanges, school study visits, sports & culture camps and bilateral school delegations — bringing Chinese students and educators to Spain, and Spanish groups to China.

What we run

Four kinds of programmes, designed with you

China → Spain

⇄

Study-abroad exchanges

Short-term study-abroad placements at Spanish partner universities — for Chinese students and recent graduates who want a taste of academic life in Spain before committing to a full degree.

China → Spain

⌂

School study visits & research camps

Structured visits for Chinese school and university groups to Spanish institutions — combining classes, campus tours, faculty meetings and cultural immersion, with the academic content designed together with the host.

China ↔ Spain

⚽

Sports & culture summer camps

Residential summer programmes in Spain that combine language classes with sport training, cultural workshops and excursions — built for school-age groups and university teams.

Spain / abroad → China

🤝

Bilateral school delegations

Reciprocal visits between Spanish and Chinese partner schools — Spanish groups travelling to China, Chinese groups travelling to Spain — designed to deepen institutional ties and often lead to longer-term cooperation.

What we handle

You propose the group — we arrange the rest

✓

Academic content

Language classes, lectures and workshops arranged with the host institution — with certificates on completion where appropriate.

✓

Logistics

Flights, accommodation, transport, insurance and local guides — planned by one team that knows both countries.

✓

People on the ground

Our Beijing head office and Madrid office, with resident staff in major Chinese cities and a resident coordinator in Barcelona, mean every group has a local contact who speaks the language.

Who takes part

From first taste of Spain to lasting partnership

Chinese schools & universities

  • A first, low-risk step into international cooperation for their students
  • Teacher training and immersion visits in Spanish-speaking environments
  • Often the starting point for a formal exchange or joint programme

Spanish institutions

  • Host inbound groups from China through a partner that handles recruitment and logistics
  • Raise your profile in China — every visiting group goes home as an ambassador
  • A soft entry into deeper cooperation with Chinese counterparts
