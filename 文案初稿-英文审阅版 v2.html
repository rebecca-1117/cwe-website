③ 公司简介 About About 页核心段

新  Founded in Beijing in 2011, Beijing Zhongxiqiao Education (CWE — Chinese and Western Exchanges) is one of China's earliest consultancies focused exclusively on cooperation between China and Spain. Headquartered in Beijing, with an office in Madrid and a resident coordinator in Barcelona, we work on both sides of the relationship — with Spanish universities seeking qualified Chinese students, and with Chinese institutions building international programmes.
中西桥教育于 2011 年创立于北京，是中国最早专注中西教育合作的机构之一。总部设在北京，马德里设有欧洲办公室，巴塞罗那有常驻协调老师——桥的两端都有我们的人。（"最早之一"替"首家"；萨拉曼卡/瓦伦西亚按你指示暂不写入）
新  Unlike general study-abroad agencies, we concentrate on Spain alone. This focus lets us know the Spanish higher-education system deeply — and today we serve as the China-based admissions office for several leading Spanish universities, including the University of Salamanca and the Autonomous University of Barcelona. We also pioneered the CAU university-pathway programme, a preparatory course that takes Chinese students from school-level Spanish to university readiness.
与综合型中介不同，我们只深耕西班牙。这份专注让我们对西班牙高教体系足够了解，如今是萨拉曼卡大学、巴塞罗那自治大学等多所西班牙名校的在华招生机构；我们还首创了 CAU 大学预科课程，把学生的语言水平从中学阶段带到大学入学要求。
Our four service lines cover cooperative education, exclusive master's cohorts, admissions at every level, and study visits and short programmes. Across China, we promote these programmes through a nationwide network of partner schools and institutions.
我们的四大服务线覆盖：中外合作办学、定制硕士班、本硕申请送生、访学游学营；在中国，通过覆盖全国的院校与机构网络推广这些项目。
新  Since 2011 we have helped nearly 10,000 students begin their studies in Spain, with a 99% visa success rate across our applicants. Ninety percent of our team have studied abroad, and many of our leadership have lived and worked in Spain. We believe strong cooperation is built on trust, transparency and long-term commitment.
成立至今，我们已帮助近 10,000 名学生赴西班牙留学，申请人签证成功率 99%。团队 90% 拥有海外学习经历，管理层多有西班牙学习与工作背景。我们相信，稳固的合作建立在信任、透明与长期承诺之上。
