Why a cohort model works

Better for students. Better for the university.

Students learn with peers

Arriving and studying as a cohort means shared support, shared experience and a smoother transition into Spanish academic life.

Universities plan with certainty

A full, pre-recruited cohort gives the faculty a predictable intake — no per-student marketing costs in China.

Quality is protected

Because students are selected and supported by one accountable team, dropout and complaint rates stay low.
