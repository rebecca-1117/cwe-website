Who we are

A China-based team focused on higher education abroad

Founded in Beijing in 2011, Beijing Zhongxiqiao Education Consulting is one of China's earliest consultancies focused on Spain and other European countries. Headquartered in Beijing, with our Madrid office and resident staff across major Chinese cities, plus a resident coordinator in Barcelona — we work on both sides of the bridge: helping universities abroad enter the Chinese market and connect with Chinese institutions, while opening more study opportunities and pathways for Chinese students overseas.
