Visits that turn agreements into relationships

A few of the moments from our in-person work with partner universities — meetings, signings and joint sessions in their own spaces.

Partnerships come in different depths

01

Authorised admissions agency

For universities seeking qualified Chinese students, we act as the authorised admissions agent in China — recruitment, guidance and student communications on the university's behalf.

02

Preparatory pathways (CAU)

We pioneered the CAU university-preparatory pathway, taking Chinese students from school-level Spanish to university readiness at partner universities.

03

In-country representation

From Beijing and Madrid — with resident staff in major Chinese cities and a resident coordinator in Barcelona — we support both sides: Chinese students in Spain, and Spanish institutions entering the Chinese market with a local partner on the ground.

04

Cooperative-education projects

Beyond admissions, we help Spanish universities and Chinese institutions design joint programmes and cooperative-education projects. Specific programmes are shared in conversation.
