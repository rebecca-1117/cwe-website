Option A · 完整版（推荐）
插入位置：cooperative-education.html 主体靠前，作为对模式有需求的读者的入门解释

The "4+0" model — students complete the full programme in China, at a partner institution, with the curriculum, faculty oversight and degree awarded jointly by the Spanish university and its Chinese partner. The Spanish partner contributes programme design, quality assurance and part of the teaching — typically in intensive blocks and online — without needing a permanent campus in China. The "0" is the years students spend abroad, but the degree is built around Spanish standards from day one.

"4+0" 模式：学生在中国合作院校完成全部 4 年本科，"0" 代表不需赴西班牙，但课程、师资、学位由西班牙大学与中方共同设计、共同授予。外方只需贡献项目设计、质量监督与部分教学（通常是密集授课模块 + 在线），无须在中国自建校区。从第一天起学位就按西班牙标准建。

对您（外方大学）的吸引力：
· 无须在中国设立实体校区，规避合规与基建投入
· 直接使用中方合作院校既有师资与设施，启动周期短
· 学位品牌延伸至中国市场；稳定的生源 + 学费分成
· 联合质量监督确保学位含金量与本部一致
Option B · 简短版

"4+0" means students study in China, but the degree is jointly delivered by the Spanish university and its Chinese partner. The Spanish partner contributes the curriculum, quality assurance and a share of teaching — without setting up a permanent campus in China.

"4+0" 即学生在中国完成本科，但学位由西外方大学与中方合作院校共同授予。外方负责课程体系、质量监督与部分教学，无须在中国自建校区。

Exclusive master's cohorts for Chinese students — a recognised, existing master's programme at a partner university, opened as a dedicated intake for a group of Chinese students. The university does not need to launch a new programme: a self-contained cohort is created out of the existing degree, designed and recruited by Zhongxiqiao together with the university.

为中国学生单独开设的硕士班级：基于合作大学 已正式开设的硕士项目，单独为中国学生开班。学校无需从零研发新项目——只需从现有学位里划出一个独立 cohort，由中西桥与校方共同设计、共同招生。

Each cohort is taught in either Spanish or English, depending on the programme. Class size, schedule and academic calendar are agreed with the host university — and the cohort is treated as a full intake, with the same degree awarded as to any other student of the programme.

每个 cohort 根据项目需要使用西语或英语授课。班级规模、课表与学年安排与校方共同商定，学位与该项目其他学生完全一致——中国学生与其他学生拿同一个学位。

首批此类 cohort 已在多所合作大学落地，包括 UAB、EAE 商学院、UCJC（卡米洛·何塞·塞拉大学）。
