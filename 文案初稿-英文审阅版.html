③ 公司简介 About About 页核心段

Founded in 2011 and headquartered in Beijing, Beijing Zhongxiqiao Education (CWE — Chinese and Western Exchanges) is an education group focused exclusively on cooperation between China and Spain. With offices in Beijing, Madrid and Barcelona, we work on both sides of the relationship — with Spanish universities seeking qualified Chinese students, and with Chinese institutions building international programmes.
中西桥教育成立于 2011 年，总部位于北京，是一家专注中国与西班牙教育合作的机构。我们在北京、马德里、巴塞罗那设有办公室，同时服务于桥的两端：为西班牙高校输送优质中国生源，为中国院校搭建国际化项目。
Unlike general study-abroad agencies, we concentrate on one country only. This focus lets us know the Spanish higher-education system deeply and act as authorised representatives for universities including the University of Salamanca and the Autonomous University of Barcelona. Our four service lines cover cooperative education, tailor-made master's cohorts, undergraduate and postgraduate admissions, and study visits and short programmes.
与综合型留学中介不同，我们只深耕西班牙一个国家。这份专注让我们对西班牙高教体系足够深入，并成为萨拉曼卡大学、巴塞罗那自治大学等高校的授权代表。我们的四大服务线覆盖：中外合作办学、定制硕士班、本硕博申请送生、访学游学营。
Ninety percent of our team have studied abroad, and our students consistently achieve a 99% visa success rate. We have worked with nearly 100 universities and colleges in China and Spain, and we believe strong cooperation is built on trust, transparency and long-term commitment.
我们的团队 90% 拥有海外学习经历，学生签证成功率稳定在 99%。我们与中西两国近 100 所院校建立过合作。我们相信，稳固的合作建立在信任、透明与长期承诺之上。

1 · Cooperative education 中外合作办学

We help Spanish universities and Chinese institutions design, launch and run cooperative programmes — from joint colleges to credit-recognition pathways.
协助西班牙高校与中国院校设计、落地、运营合作项目——从联合学院到学分互认。
Joint colleges with Chinese universities (e.g. engineering-intelligence and health-science directions)
与中国高校共建联合学院（如工程智能、健康科学方向）——参考：USAL、UAX 共建项目
Curriculum co-design and credit recognition between partner institutions
课程共建与学分互认
End-to-end project operations support, from approval to first cohort
从申报到首届开班的全程运营支持

2 · Tailor-made master's cohorts 定制硕士班级

Full-cohort master's programmes designed for Chinese students at our partner universities — taught in English, with structured progression and in-class support in Chinese.
在合作大学为中国学生开设的整班定制硕士项目——英语授课、结构化学制、课堂配备中文支持。
Programmes accessible to three-year college (专科) graduates, no national exam required
专科毕业生可申、无需统考
English-taught, one-year structured pathways
英语授课、一年制结构化路径
Cohorts designed with the university around Chinese students' needs
与中国学生需求匹配的专班设计（参考：EAE 2025 起硕士专班、UCJC）

3 · Study-abroad admissions 留学申请送生

Guided admissions for Chinese students to Spanish universities — undergraduate, master's and doctoral level, from application to arrival.
协助中国学生申请西班牙大学本、硕、博——从申请到落地全程指导。
University and programme selection matched to each student's profile
按学生背景精准匹配院校与专业
Applications, CAU university-preparatory pathway, visas and pre-departure guidance
申请、CAU 预科路径、签证与行前指导
99% visa success rate across our applicants
申请人签证成功率 99%

4 · Study visits & short programmes 访学 · 游学营

Structured short programmes that bring students and teachers to Spain — and Spanish students to China.
结构化短期项目，带中国师生走进西班牙，也带西班牙学生来到中国。
Short-term exchange and study-visit programmes with partner universities (e.g. UAB)
与伙伴大学合作的短期交流与访学项目（如 UAB 短期交流生）
Spanish language & culture camps and study tours
西语语言文化营、研学团
Bilateral visits — Chinese delegations to Spain and Spanish groups to China
双向访问：中方团赴西、西语国家学生/教师团访华
